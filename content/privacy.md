## The short version

This is a static website. It sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics**, and
embeds **no third-party scripts or trackers**. Fonts are served from this
domain, not from a third-party font CDN.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.). When you open a page,
your browser necessarily sends technical data to GitHub's servers — including
your IP address, the requested URL, the time of the request, and your browser's
user agent. GitHub processes this to deliver the site and to protect against
abuse. See GitHub's own privacy documentation for details on that processing.

## Contact by email

If you email me, your message and address are used solely to answer you.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to information, correction, deletion,
restriction of processing, data portability, and to object. To exercise any of
these, use the contact address in the [legal notice](/legal-notice/).
